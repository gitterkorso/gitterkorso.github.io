Conceptual Bridge Design Project

Klart VT 2026 AF2201 Bridge Design Revit · AutoCAD

Suspension Bridge design: project in the course AF2201 Bridge Design. The task was present a conceptual bridge with a main span of 675 meters. The project included calculations for the main cables, hangers, and deck. The project also involved creating detailed drawings and a 3D model of the bridge using Revit and AutoCAD. The design focused on ensuring structural integrity and aesthetic appeal.
